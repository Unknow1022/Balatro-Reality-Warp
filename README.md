## Notes & Disclaimer

* **Origins & Evolution:** The earliest prototype versions of this mod were originally titled **"Cracklatro"** and later **"Wichter Brew Expansion"**.
* **Experimental Spirit:** This mod is designed fundamentally as an **experiment and creative playground** rather than a strictly competitive, vanilla-balanced expansion, This was Vibe Coded in certain parts of the proyect.

---

## Requirements & Installation

1. Install **[Steamodded](https://github.com/Steamodded/smods)** (`v1.0.0+`) and **[Lovely](https://github.com/ethangreen-dev/lovely-injector)**.
2. Place this mod folder into your Balatro mods directory:
   - **Windows:** `%AppData%/Balatro/Mods/RealityWarp`
3. *(Optional)* Compatible with **[JokerDisplay](https://github.com/nhandev/JokerDisplay)** and **[CardSleeves](https://github.com/larswijn/CardSleeves)**.

---

## Features at a Glance

* **60+ Custom Jokers:** Commons, Uncommons, Rares, Legendaries, and transcendent **Outsiders** with game-warping abilities.
* **Warp Portal & Tags:** Summon elusive Outsiders directly from the void.
* **Kyra's Black Market & Dark Money (EUR):** Alternative illicit market featuring exclusive Potions, Spectrals, and stolen Jokers.
* **Battle of Gods Mode:** Post-Ante 8 pantheon showdowns with cosmic boss entities and leveling Nursery Familiars.
* **Potion Pouch:** Dedicated `[ POUCH ]` HUD storing craftable alchemical brews without using standard consumable slots.
* **Custom Decks & Sleeves:** Thematic starting setups designed for unorthodox strategies.
